## Script

**Cold open.** Dana and Marisol send the same email. One is in English. Does the sorter treat them the same?

**The problem.** An agency’s inbox gets Spanish and mixed-language emails. If Jev is less sure about them, they can miss deadlines or wait in the catch-all queue, and language can stand in for national origin.

**How it works.** TypeSafe says English is Jev’s main training language and where it’s most accurate, and that other languages are handled but not equally well. It publishes no numbers by language and expects 1.14 to improve here.

**The example.** Split your past emails by language, count misses for each group, and send unclear non-English emails to bilingual staff at the same priority.

**Close.** Language is only for getting the email to someone who can read it, never for anything else. We haven’t run this yet; when we do, we’ll publish everything.
