## Script

**Cold open.** A customer’s claim report has her name, her policy number, her phone number and her swollen wrist. The router needs one of those.

**The problem.** Every request to Jev sends the text to TypeSafe. A first notice of loss carries personal and sometimes health details.

**How it works.** TypeSafe says it doesn’t train on your inputs, hosts in the U.S. and offers a SOC 2 Type II report on request. Its standard retention promise is “as long as reasonably necessary,” and zero retention is an enterprise option you have to ask for.

**The example.** Strip names, numbers and contact details before sending, run your test set both ways to make sure routing doesn’t change, and take five written questions to TypeSafe.
