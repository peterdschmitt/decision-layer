## Script

**Cold open.** “A branch from my neighbor’s oak flattened our fence. Is that covered?” The answer is somewhere in a forty-page policy.

**The problem.** Your service person has to find the right sentences fast, and know when the policy is silent.

**How it works.** Every line gets a number. Jev points to the lines that answer the question. A second yes/no question asks whether any line answers it at all, because the pointing always lands somewhere.

**The example.** The fence question lands on the tree, fence and windstorm lines. The neighbor’s-side question gets a low yes/no, so the screen says the policy doesn’t address it.

**Close.** Jev points; your licensed person explains; the carrier decides. We haven’t run this yet; when we do, we’ll publish everything.
